# Member portal details

This ZIP is structured for extraction into the root of your existing moba-site repository. It replaces `src/pages/Member.jsx`.

After a member signs in, the portal now shows the register details available for that account: name, email, phone, cohort, house, MOBA number, status, and subscription paid-through date when recorded. It also links to the details-correction form. The pre-login screen remains private and does not reveal member information.

Extract to the repository root, build, commit, push, and redeploy. No database migration is needed.
